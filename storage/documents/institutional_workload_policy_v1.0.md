# UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC WORKLOAD POLICY REGULATIONS (2026-27)
## Document ID: DOC-POL-2026-V1
### Approved Institutional Workload Policy - Synthetic University Dataset, Version 1.0

### Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This regulation governs the allocation, computation, and maximum caps for academic teaching hours across all university departments (Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and Data Science). All figures apply weekly to full-time academic faculty.

### Section 3: Teaching Hour Computation Rules
Teaching hours are calculated strictly by scheduled duration in the institutional timetable:
- **RULE-001 (Lecture):** A 1-hour lecture contributes 1 workload hour. Calculation Method: Actual duration in hours. Status: Included.
- **RULE-002 (Tutorial):** A 1-hour tutorial contributes 1 workload hour. Calculation Method: Actual duration in hours. Status: Included.
- **RULE-003 (Laboratory):** For this synthetic dataset, lab hours count at actual duration; no multiplier. Calculation Method: Actual duration in hours. Status: Included.
- **RULE-004 (Project Supervision):** Tracked as an assignment/responsibility, not scheduled teaching hours. Calculation Method: Not in timetable workload. Status: Excluded.
- **RULE-005 (Exam Invigilation):** Tracked separately as examination duty. Calculation Method: Not in weekly teaching hours. Status: Excluded.
- **RULE-006 (Cancelled Session):** Do not count cancelled sessions. Calculation Method: Cancelled session. Status: Excluded.
- **RULE-007 (Substituted Session):** Original faculty is not credited; substitute faculty receives the scheduled duration. Calculation Method: Count against substitute faculty. Status: Included.


### Section 4: Policy Exceptions and Approval Process
1. **Temporary Workload Overrides:** A faculty member may be allocated teaching hours exceeding the standard maximum cap only if a formal policy exception has been officially APPROVED by the Dean/Registrar prior to the evaluation week.
2. **Pending and Rejected Exceptions:** Applications with status 'Pending' or 'Rejected' do NOT override the policy maximum limit under any circumstances.
3. **Validity Period:** Approved exceptions are strictly valid only between their valid_from and valid_to dates.

### Section 5: Workload Exemptions and Administrative Relief
1. **Relief on Minimum Workload:** Faculty holding administrative responsibilities (such as Head of Department - HOD) receive approved relief hours per week reducing their minimum expected teaching commitment.
2. **Maximum Cap Invariance:** Workload exemptions do NOT raise the maximum teaching-hour cap. The standard maximum limit remains operative unless an approved policy exception exists.

### Section 6: Non-Teaching Academic Responsibilities
Academic responsibilities including Question Paper Preparation, Internal Assessment Evaluation, Project Supervision, and Examination Invigilation are tracked separately as assignments and are excluded from weekly timetable teaching-hour calculations.
